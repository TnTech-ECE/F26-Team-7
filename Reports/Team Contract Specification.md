## **Introduction**

*This teamwork contract outlines the agreed-upon expectations, responsibilities, and guidelines for effective collaboration among the members of Team 7. By adhering to this contract, we aim to foster a productive, respectful, and supportive team environment conducive to the success of our project.*


## **Team Expectations**

In this section, the team will discuss and clearly define their collective expectations in various key areas. Be specific to ensure mutual understanding.

### **1. Participation** *(Engagement and Effort in the Project)*

- **Expectation:** Each team member is expected to:
  - Actively participate in all team meetings and discussions.
  - Engage fully in project activities and contribute ideas.
  - Notify the team in advance if unable to attend a meeting or meet an obligation.

### **2. Contribution** *(Progress on the Project)*

- **Expectation:** Each team member is expected to:
  - Complete assigned tasks diligently and on time.
  - Contribute equal effort to the project's progress.
  - Share expertise and assist others when needed.

### **3. Communication**

- **Expectation:** Team communication should be:
  - Professional, respectful, and considerate.
  - Conducted through agreed-upon channels (notion for outside hours, group chat for working hours).
	- Responsive, with replies to inquiries within 24 hours on weekdays for texts within the week for notion.
	- check notion once a week (at meetings)
	- emergency meetings can be performed digitaly.
  - commounication Should be kept during regular working hours on weekdays (9-5)

### **4. Meetings**

- **Expectation:** Regarding team meetings:
  - **Frequency:** The team will meet once a week on Mondays 3-4:15.
  - **Attendance:** Members are expected to attend all meetings; absences should be communicated in advance.
  - emergency meetings can be performed digitaly
  - **Preparation:** Members should come prepared, having completed any agreed-upon tasks or research.

### **5. Conduct**

- **Expectation:** Team members will:
  - Treat each other with respect and courtesy.
  - Listen actively and allow others to express their ideas without interruption.
  - Address disagreements professionally and constructively.

### **6. Conflict Resolution**

- **Expectation:** The team will:
  - Address conflicts promptly through open dialogue.
  - Use a step-by-step approach to resolve issues:
    1. Discuss the issue privately between involved parties.
    2. If unresolved, bring the issue to the team for discussion.
    3. Seek input from the instructor if necessary.
  - Focus on finding mutually acceptable solutions.
  - lack of contribution i.e. missed meetings or deadlines will lead to need for donuts.

### **7. Deadlines**

- **Expectation:** Regarding deadlines:
  - All team-set and instructor-set deadlines are to be met.
  - If a member anticipates difficulty meeting a deadline, they must inform the team as soon as possible.
  - The team will collaboratively adjust workloads to ensure project timelines are maintained.
  - Internal deadlines will normally be set 24 - 48 hours before instructor deadlines so there is time to review and combine everyone's work.

## **Additional Agreements**


Members should not make major design changes without informing the rest of the team. Starting with the nearest Friday.

All important project files will be stored in a shared location, such as OneDrive/Google Drive/GitHub, rather than only on one person's computer.

Code will be maintained in a "shared repository (GitHub)", and major changes should be documented.

Task assignments should identify both the responsible person and expected completion date.

Engineering decisions that significantly affect cost, requirements, architecture, or schedule should be discussed by the team before implementation.

Every member is responsible for understanding the overall design, even when work is divided into specialties.

Before reports or presentations are submitted, at least one team member other than the original author should review the material.

This document may be ammended if certified by a unanimous blind vote.
